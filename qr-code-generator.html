Tips for Reliable QR Codes

  • Test Before Printing: Scan your generated QR code with two different phone cameras or scanner apps to verify it opens smoothly.
  • Keep Links Clean: Shorter URLs create less dense QR patterns that scan faster even at smaller print sizes.
  • Maintain Contrast: Keep dark QR modules on a light background and avoid stretching or cropping the border.

Common Use Cases

QR codes are widely used on business cards, menus, event flyers, product packaging, classroom handouts, and quick landing pages.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of text can I encode?
You can enter website links starting with https://, phone numbers, short notes, or identifiers.

Do QR codes expire on their own?
Static text or direct URL data encoded in the QR code does not expire unless the destination link itself is removed.
