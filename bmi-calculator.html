Understanding BMI Categories

Body Mass Index (BMI) compares your weight in kilograms to your height in meters squared. Standard adult categories are:

  • Underweight: BMI below 18.5
  • Normal weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25.0 to 29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30.0 or higher

Fitness & Lifestyle Notes

BMI is a helpful general screening tool, though it does not directly measure body fat percentage, muscle mass, or bone density. Pair regular movement, balanced nutrition, and adequate sleep with guidance from a healthcare professional for personal health advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What formula is used for BMI?
BMI equals weight (kg) divided by height in meters squared: weight / (height_in_meters × height_in_meters).

Does BMI account for athletic muscle?
Muscular individuals can have a higher BMI without excess body fat, so look at overall fitness and health context as well.
