How Monthly EMI Is Calculated

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) spreads principal and interest evenly over the loan tenure. When the monthly interest rate is greater than zero, standard reducing-balance EMI uses the loan principal, monthly rate (annual rate divided by 12 and 100), and total number of monthly payments.

Planning Tips Before Borrowing

  • Compare Tenure vs. Interest: Longer tenures lower monthly EMI payments, while shorter tenures usually reduce total interest paid over time.
  • Check Extra Charges: Processing fees, insurance, or late payment charges may apply separately depending on the lender.
  • 0% Installments: If an annual rate is 0%, the monthly payment is simply the principal divided by the number of months.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the interest rate is 0%?
Enter 0 in the Annual Interest Rate field and the calculator divides the total amount evenly across the selected months.

Does it show total interest and total payment?
Yes, right below the monthly EMI figure you will see total interest and total amount payable.
