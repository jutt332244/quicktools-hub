What Makes a Password Strong?

Longer passwords with a mix of character types greatly increase the number of combinations an attacker would have to try, making brute-force guesses far harder.

Password Security Best Practices

  • Aim for 12 to 16+ Characters: Length matters more than short tricks—14 to 16 characters offers strong everyday protection.
  • Never Reuse Passwords: Use a unique password for email, banking, social media, and hosting/GitHub accounts.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Pairing a strong password with an authenticator app or security key adds a vital second layer.
