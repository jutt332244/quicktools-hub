How Exact Age Is Calculated

An exact age calculator compares your date of birth against today's calendar date while accounting for varying month lengths (28, 30, or 31 days) and leap years.

Common Uses of an Age Calculator

  • Official Forms & Admissions: School admissions, job applications, and government forms often require exact age on a specific cutoff date.
  • Fitness & Health Benchmarks: Many health metrics and wellness guidelines group adults by age brackets.
  • Milestone Tracking: Checking how many days you have lived or how many days remain until your next birthday.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator account for leap years?
Yes, JavaScript's calendar date calculation automatically handles leap days in February.

Is my date of birth stored on any server?
No, all calculations run directly in your browser.
