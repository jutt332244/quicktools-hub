Common Conversion Formulas

  • Kilometers ↔ Miles: 1 km ≈ 0.621371 miles; 1 mile = 1.60934 km.
  • Kilograms ↔ Pounds: 1 kg ≈ 2.20462 lbs; 1 lb = 0.453592 kg.
  • Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32; °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.

Everyday Uses

Use this converter for travel distances, fitness tracking, shipping weights, recipes, and international weather readings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert negative temperatures?
Yes, negative values such as −10°C or −5°F work automatically.

How many decimal places are shown?
Results are rounded cleanly up to 2 decimal places for quick reading.
