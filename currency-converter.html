How Exchange Rates Work

Foreign exchange rates show how much of one currency is needed to purchase another. Rates fluctuate during active global market sessions based on supply, demand, interest rates, and economic news.

Tips Before International Transfers or Payments

  • Mid-Market vs. Bank Rate: Online rate trackers display mid-market rates, while banks or card issuers may add a small spread or service fee.
  • Compare Payment Methods: Paying in local currency when traveling or shopping abroad often gives a clearer conversion breakdown.
  • Check Timing: Currency markets move continuously across global time zones.
