Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At QuickTools Hub (operated by MOUM), your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit and use our website.

1. Browser-Based Tool Processing

All calculators, text converters, password generators, and utility tools on QuickTools Hub run locally inside your web browser. We do not require account registration, and we do not store your personal calculations or entered text on our servers.

2. Log Files & Basic Analytics

Like most websites, standard server logs may record non-identifying technical data such as browser type, device type, referring pages, and timestamps to help maintain performance and security.

3. Cookies & Google AdSense

We may use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to QuickTools Hub and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

4. Third-Party Links & APIs

Certain utilities (such as live exchange rate lookups or QR image rendering) may fetch public data from external services. Those third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Information

QuickTools Hub does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
